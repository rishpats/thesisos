# ThesisOS Demo Data & Provenance Notes

All observations in `data/demo/financial_facts.csv` are compiled from audited Ind-AS Consolidated Financial Statements published in the official Integrated Annual Reports of the respective companies via NSE corporate filings.

- **Monetary Unit:** `INR_CRORE` (1 Crore = 10,000,000 INR)
- **Share Count Unit:** `SHARES_CRORE`
- **Market Price Policy:** Reference prices in `companies.csv` are explicitly dated historical closing prices for valuation demonstration and are never presented as live exchange feeds.
